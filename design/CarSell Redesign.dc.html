1aAssumptions & how Organic maps onto a car marketplace

Warm, round, and calmer than the old app

The current app is a form portal: flat mid-green fills, six-field screens, filters stacked over filters, a five-tab bar fighting a floating button. Organic gives it a cream ground and sand cards, with a deep green for every action and verified signal and a warm ochre for time-pressure and caution — so price and bid carry the color instead of the chrome.

Green = act
Buy now, bid, submit an offer — and every verified signal. The brand voice.
Ochre = time & caution
Live auctions, countdowns, paint history, awaiting payment. Hard errors use the same hue one step deeper — a 200 fill with 900 text — so a failure never reads as a mere advisory. Never used for a normal action.
One card, one car
The old card carried eleven chips. The new one: photo, name, price, three facts.
Listing type is a decision
Direct sale, negotiation, auction — each with plain-language consequences and its own settings.
